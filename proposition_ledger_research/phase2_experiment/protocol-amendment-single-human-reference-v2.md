# Arm D single-human reference amendment

## Scope

This post-freeze amendment changes only the human provenance and lock gate for Arm D in the supplemental `market-planning-live-20260901` challenge. It does not change the case, transcript, evaluation points, replay contexts, arm definitions, prompts, model profiles, budgets, postvalidation, provider policy, no-future-turn rules, or 29-call plan.

The original gate required two independent human raters and a distinct human adjudicator. For this single supplemental case, Arm D instead requires one transcript-first human-authored reference, deterministic evidence resolution and ledger materialisation, and a hash lock completed before any experiment-generated machine output is revealed.

## Reference classification

- `arm_d_reference_type`: `single_human_reference`
- `consensus_gold`: `false`
- `machine_authorship`: `forbidden`
- `machine_output_revealed_before_lock`: `false`
- `pre_reveal_reference_lock`: `required`

The locked result is a human reference ledger. It is not adjudicated gold, consensus gold, or an independently replicated annotation.

## Human provenance

The annotator may be the investigator or user. The provenance receipt must truthfully record whether the annotator knew the working hypothesis, had previously seen the historical published replies, and was the investigator. Those facts do not invalidate the reference and must not be represented as blindness.

The annotator must remain independent of the experiment's machine representations: before the reference lock succeeds, the annotator may not inspect the Arm B summary, Arm C ledger, or any A-D downstream reply generated by this experiment. A human-authored reference may not be derived from a model- or Codex-authored ledger.

## Human and deterministic responsibilities

The human chooses propositions, issues, commitments, obligations, relations, and their exact supporting text and zero-based occurrence indexes. The human does not enter character offsets, permanent IDs, ledger hashes, or other deterministic boilerplate.

The runner may validate the completed human form, resolve exact-text selectors, derive offsets and stable identifiers, materialise the ledger and its evaluation snapshots, compute hashes, and report missing or inconsistent entries. It must reject invalid or unresolved choices and must not add, infer, select, or silently repair substantive semantic annotations.

## Execution boundary

Validation, reference locking, and preparation are offline and make zero provider calls. Live A-D execution remains fail-closed until the valid human provenance, materialised reference, and pre-reveal lock have all been verified.
