# OpenAI Quality Trial 001 Review

This ten-case pilot compares quality settings, not providers.

- Medium wins: 3
- High wins: 4
- Equal: 0
- Neither: 3
- High decisive win rate: 57.1%
- Medium known estimated spend: $0.4584
- High known estimated spend: $1.7084
- High/Medium cost ratio: 3.73x
- Medium cost per winning image: $0.1528
- High cost per winning image: $0.4271

## Interpretation

High quality is not economically justified by this ten-case pilot; retain Medium as the default research candidate pending a larger independent comparison.

High produced one more preferred image than Medium (4 versus 3), but three cases produced no acceptable image at either setting. With only seven decisive cases, the 57.1% High share is not persuasive evidence of a durable quality advantage, while its observed trial cost was materially higher.
