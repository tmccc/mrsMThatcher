# OpenAI Quality Trial Review

This 30-case pilot compares quality settings, not providers.

- Medium wins: 6
- High wins: 7
- Equal: 2
- Neither: 15
- High decisive win rate: 53.8%
- Medium known estimated spend: $1.3786
- High known estimated spend: $5.1286
- High/Medium cost ratio: 3.72x
- Medium cost per winning image: $0.2298
- High cost per winning image: $0.7327

## Interpretation

High quality is not economically justified by this bounded pilot; retain Medium as the default research candidate pending stronger independent evidence.

High's net win advantage was +1 across 13 decisive cases. This must be interpreted against its materially higher cost and the 15/30 neither rate.
